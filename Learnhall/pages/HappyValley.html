Happy Valley, Oregon

A Beacon of Education and Community

Happy Valley, Oregon, a rapidly growing suburb of Portland, is celebrated for its scenic beauty, family-friendly atmosphere, and exceptional educational opportunities. With a population of approximately 26,000, this vibrant city combines a rich history with a forward-looking community, making it an ideal place for students to thrive. A Glimpse into Happy Valley’s History

Happy Valley’s roots trace back to the 1850s, when settlers like Christian Deardorff established farms in the fertile Clackamas County. Incorporated in 1965, the city was named for its lush, picturesque landscape, which fostered a sense of optimism among early residents. Initially a rural farming community, Happy Valley evolved into a suburban hub within Oregon’s “Silicon Forest,” with proximity to tech giants like Intel. Landmarks like the Deardorff Valley and the city’s annual Fun in the Park event reflect its heritage and community spirit.

Our tutoring service at Learnhall.com aligns with Happy Valley’s growth, providing innovative, high-quality academic support to empower students in this dynamic environment.

The Educational Landscape of Happy Valley

Happy Valley is primarily served by the North Clackamas School District (NCSD), one of Oregon’s top-performing districts, enrolling approximately 16,700 students across 31 schools, including 22 elementary schools, four middle schools, three high schools, and three charter schools, as of the 2024-2025 school year. NCSD ranks highly, with 34% of students proficient in math and 46% in reading, compared to state averages of 31% and 44%. Happy Valley Elementary School, with 489 students, ranks in the top 20% of Oregon elementary schools, boasting 47% math proficiency and 52% reading proficiency. Happy Valley Middle School, serving 1,021 students, ranks #52 among Oregon middle schools, with 36% math and 54% reading proficiency, and is praised for its engaging teachers and activities.

Clackamas High School, serving 1,224 students, is a standout in Happy Valley, ranking #51 among Oregon high schools with a 92% graduation rate, surpassing the state’s 81%. It offers robust programs like Advanced Placement (AP) courses and an acclaimed women’s basketball team, though arts funding lags behind sports. Clackamas Middle College, a charter school with 270 students, excels with 34% math proficiency and 85% reading proficiency, ranking in the top 30% of Oregon charter schools. Parents praise its college-readiness classes and field trips, such as visits to Salem’s state capitol.

Private school options in Happy Valley are limited, but nearby schools like Valley Christian School (150 students) and La Salle Catholic College Preparatory (700 students) in Milwaukie serve families seeking faith-based or specialized education. Oregon’s private school enrollment is about 10%, with Happy Valley families contributing a small fraction due to NCSD’s strength.

Homeschooling in Happy Valley

A Growing Choice

Homeschooling is flourishing in Happy Valley, reflecting Oregon’s active homeschooling community. In 2024-2025, about 7.03% of Oregon’s K-12 students (39,000) are homeschooled, up from 6.53% the previous year, with an estimated 22,000 in the Portland metro area, including Happy Valley. Nationally, 6.73% of students (3.7 million) are homeschooled, a 51% increase over six years. Oregon’s homeschoolers score at the 71st to 80th percentile on standardized tests, 15-30 points above public school peers. The COVID-19 pandemic drove a 70% surge in 2020-2021, with families citing concerns about school environments (83%) and instructional quality (41%).

Oregon requires homeschoolers to notify the Northwest Regional Education Service District and test in grades 3, 5, 8, and 10 after an 18-month grace period. Happy Valley families access resources like the Happy Valley Library’s STEM and literacy programs and Village Home in Beaverton, serving over 400 learners with flexible classes. Microschools and co-ops support specialized needs like dyslexia.
