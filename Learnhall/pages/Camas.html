Camas, WA: A Premier Community for Education and Growth

Nestled along the banks of the Columbia River, Camas, Washington, is a thriving city known for its scenic beauty, strong sense of community, and exceptional schools. With a population of over 27,000 residents, Camas offers a perfect blend of small-town charm and modern amenities, making it an ideal place for families and students. At LearnHall, we’re proud to support the educational needs of Camas students—whether they attend public schools, private institutions, or are homeschooled. Since 2014, we’ve provided high-quality, affordable tutoring in any subject or exam, both in-person and online, helping students achieve their academic goals.

A Brief History of Camas

Camas was originally home to the Chinook Native American tribe before European settlers arrived in the early 19th century. The city’s name comes from the camas lily, a plant with blue flowers that was a vital food source for indigenous peoples. In the late 1800s, Camas became a hub for the paper industry, with the Camas Paper Mill (now owned by Georgia-Pacific) playing a major role in the local economy.

Over time, Camas evolved from a mill town into a vibrant suburban community, attracting families with its top-rated schools, low crime rate, and high quality of life. Today, it’s recognized as one of the best places to live in Washington State, offering a mix of outdoor recreation, cultural events, and excellent educational opportunities.

Camas Schools: Excellence in Education

One of the biggest draws for families moving to Camas is its outstanding school district. The Camas School District (CSD) consistently ranks among the best in the state, with high graduation rates, strong test scores, and a commitment to student success.

Public Schools in Camas

Camas High School

(~1,800 students)

Known for its rigorous academics, award-winning extracurricular programs, and high college acceptance rates.

Hayes Freedom High School

(~150 students)

An alternative high school offering personalized learning experiences.

Liberty Middle School & Skyridge Middle School

Both serve grades 6-8 and are highly regarded for their STEM and arts programs.

Multiple Elementary Schools

(Grass Valley, Dorothy Fox, Helen Baller, etc.)

Each emphasizes foundational skills in reading, math, and science.

The district boasts a 94% graduation rate, well above the national average, and many students go on to attend prestigious universities.
