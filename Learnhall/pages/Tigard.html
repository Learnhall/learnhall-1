Tigard, Oregon:

A Hub of Learning and Community

Tigard, Oregon, a dynamic suburb southwest of Portland, is renowned for its blend of urban convenience, small-town charm, and exceptional educational opportunities. With a population of approximately 56,000, Tigard is a vibrant part of Oregon’s “Silicon Forest,” offering a rich historical backdrop and a family-friendly environment. Since 2014, our tutoring service at Learnhall.com, backed by numerous 5-star Google reviews, has proudly served Tigard’s residents, providing personalized, affordable academic support for any course or exam, available in-person or online.

A Glimpse into Tigard’s History

Tigard’s history began in the 1850s, named after settler Wilson Tigard, whose family established a donation land claim in 1852. Incorporated in 1961, the city grew from a farming community into a commercial hub with the arrival of the Oregon Electric Railway in 1908. The historic downtown, with its revitalized Main Street, reflects Tigard’s roots, while landmarks like the John Tigard House, built in 1880, preserve its heritage. Today, Tigard thrives as a tech and retail center, home to Washington Square Mall and close to companies like Nike. Our tutoring service at Learnhall.com supports this progressive community, offering high-quality, flexible academic assistance to empower Tigard’s students in a competitive landscape.

The Educational Landscape of Tigard

Tigard is served by the Tigard-Tualatin School District (TTSD), the ninth-largest in Oregon, enrolling 11,656 students across 18 schools: 11 elementary schools, three middle schools, three high schools, and one charter school, as of the 2024-2025 school year. TTSD ranks #12 in Oregon, with 47% of students proficient in math and 65% in reading, surpassing state averages of 31% and 44%. Tigard High School, with 1,782 students, offers distinctive programs like International Baccalaureate (IB), AVID college prep, and computer-aided design (CAD), with a 90% graduation rate. However, its 33% math and 54% reading proficiency indicate areas for growth. Parents praise its teachers but note inconsistent food quality and communication, with one review stating, “Teachers care deeply, but the school pushes ideals not all students share.”

Elementary schools like Mary Woodward Elementary (360 students) rank in the top 20% of Oregon schools, with 50% math and 60% reading proficiency. Fowler Middle School (918 students) earns an A-minus on Niche, with 44% math and 62% reading proficiency, and is lauded for its supportive staff. MITCH Charter School, serving 244 K-5 students, emphasizes hands-on learning with agricultural and social-emotional programs, earning a 3.9-star parent rating for its “strong academics and community feel.” A 2022 $723 million bond supports facility upgrades, ensuring modern learning spaces. Private schools in Tigard include St. Anthony School (K-8, ~200 students) and Westside Christian School (K-12, ~400 students), offering faith-based education. Oregon’s private school enrollment is ~10%, with Tigard families contributing modestly due to TTSD’s strength.

Homeschooling in Tigard

A Growing Choice

Homeschooling is thriving in Tigard, reflecting Oregon’s vibrant homeschooling community. In 2024-2025, ~7.03% of Oregon’s K-12 students (39,000) are homeschooled, up from 6.53% the previous year, with ~22,000 in the Portland metro area, including Tigard. Nationally, 6.73% of students (3.7 million) are homeschooled, a 51% increase over six years. Oregon’s homeschoolers score at the 71st-80th percentile on standardized tests, 15-30 points above public school peers. The COVID-19 pandemic drove a 70% surge in 2020-2021, with families citing school environment concerns (83%) and instructional quality (41%).

Oregon requires homeschoolers to notify the Northwest Regional Education Service District and test in grades 3, 5, 8, and 10 after an 18-month grace period. Tigard families access resources like the Tigard Public Library’s STEM and literacy programs and Village Home in Beaverton, serving over 400 learners with flexible classes. The Tigard-Tualatin Online Academy offers supplemental courses for homeschoolers.
